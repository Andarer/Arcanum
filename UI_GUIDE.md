# Arcanum UI Guide & Visual Effects Specification v2.4

## Visual FX Engine & Animation Guidelines
- **Particle Atmosphere Canvas (`arcanum-atmosphere.js`)**: Continuous ambient embers, floating light beams, and pointer click ripples.
- **Procedural Sound Effects (`arcanum-audio.js`)**: WebAudio frequency synthesis for button taps, card flips, and combat strikes.
- **Cinematic Transitions (`ArcanumCinematics`)**: GPU-accelerated screen shake, full-screen mode, wake lock, zoom/fade/slide screen transitions.
- **Spatial Focus Ring**: High-contrast glow outlines on focused elements for TV and controller navigation.
